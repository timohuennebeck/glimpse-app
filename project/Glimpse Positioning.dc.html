Positioning note · Glimpse

What Glimpse is that Locket isn't

Locket owns the surface. BeReal owned the ritual and lost it. The opening is the thing neither of them has: a homescreen that asks something of you.

Where the category actually is

Locket drifted into chat

Its own reviewers describe it as a chat app now: reactions, DMs, weekly roll calls, verified celebrities. The widget became an entry point to a messenger, and Snapchat already wins that fight.

BeReal proved the prompt works, then wore it out

Roughly 20M daily users in late 2022 down to about 6M by spring 2023, sold to Voodoo in 2024. One obligatory post a day is an event, but it has nowhere to live after it's posted.

The clones lose on trust, not features

Memento and friends rank 4.5 stars with review threads full of broken sync, friend caps and instant paywalls. Even Locket's top complaints are paying to customise the widget and a rewind that loses months.

Sources: Business of Apps and Wikipedia (BeReal users, Voodoo acquisition), App Store and JustUseApp review threads for Locket and Memento, Sept 2026.

The diagnosis

Locket: a surface without a loop

A photo sits on your homescreen until someone replaces it. Nothing is asked of the person looking at it.

BeReal: a loop without a surface

A timer creates a real obligation, then buries the result in a feed you have to open to see.

Glimpse's opening: put the obligation on the surface. The widget is not a frame, it's a request.

Three ways to claim that

A Trade to see Recommended core

Their photo lands on your homescreen frosted, with their name on it. Tap it and the camera opens. The second you send one back, both photos unlock — on your widget and theirs, side by side. No sending without receiving, no looking without sending.

Beats Locket because

Locket cannot copy it without breaking the passive glance it sells.

TikTok hook

The unlock is a screen recording: blur, camera, reveal. Three seconds, no explanation needed.

Risk

Withholding can feel punitive. Needs a soft escape: everything unlocks by itself after some hours.

B Same minute

The BeReal part, but private and on the homescreen. Once a day the widget itself starts a countdown for you and your people at the same time. What you get back is a pair taken in the same minute, in two different places.

Beats Locket because

Simultaneity is what makes a photo of nothing worth looking at.

TikTok hook

The pair is the post. Long distance, two cities, one minute.

Risk

A daily forced window is exactly what burned BeReal out. Better weekly, or opt-in per pair.

C Something to keep

One shot, no retake, no camera roll upload. Everything you traded comes back at the end of the month as a film strip you can export, or order printed. This is the retention and revenue layer, not the headline.

Beats Locket because

Locket's rewind is its most complained-about feature. People are asking to keep their year and can't.

TikTok hook

Unboxing the print. The artefact does the advertising.

Risk

Physical fulfilment is a different company. Start with the export.

My recommendation

Locket is a window into someone's day. Glimpse is a window that only opens both ways.

Ship A as the whole product. Add B as a weekly event once pairs are active, and C at the first month mark. One sentence in the store listing, one mechanic in the app, one thing to film.

What to cut

Open chat, large friend lists, feed-style browsing. That is the road Locket took and it ends at Snapchat. Also do not paywall widget customisation — it is the single most-hated thing about Locket, and it is what your users would otherwise show off for free.

TikTok

The app has to produce its own ad. Every video is a screen recording of the unlock or a pair of photos — never a founder explaining a feature.

The unlock, POV

Homescreen, frosted tile with a name on it, hesitation, camera, reveal. This is the hero format and it is three seconds long.

Homescreen setup

Ride the customisation genre that already exists on TikTok. Your widget has to look good next to a themed homescreen, so ship layouts and frames free.

Distance stories

Long distance, exchange year, a best friend who moved. Emotional POV over product demo, creator's own footage, your widget as the punchline.

Refuse-to-trade bit

The comedy the mechanic writes itself: the friend who leaves you frosted for two days. Duet and stitch bait, and it teaches the rule.

Seed narrow

German-speaking first, since the app already is. Fifteen to twenty micro creators inside specific circles — Fernbeziehung, Abi year groups, Auslandsjahr, one university city at a time. Pairs, not reach: the app is worthless to one person alone.

The number to watch

Not installs. The share of new users who complete a first trade with a real friend inside 24 hours, and invites sent per install. Locket clones die because one half of the pair never arrives.

Open, and worth deciding before more screens

01Is Glimpse for one person, a pair, or a small crew? The trade mechanic reads very differently for a couple than for six friends.
02Does an unsent trade expire, or does it unlock on its own? This decides whether the app feels playful or nagging.
03The paywall sits at step 10 of onboarding today. If the growth loop is pairs, the first trade has to happen before anyone is asked for money.
